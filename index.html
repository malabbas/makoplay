About Makoplay

Makoplay was born from a simple truth: people don't want to fill forms, they want to win.

The idea: help brands and small businesses turn an offer into a short, branded mini-game. A future campaign builder would use AI to tailor the game, let marketers set a prize and winner limit, and give players a shareable link to join and play.

This prototype explores the product story and signup experience. The linked Pizza Rush game makes the idea tangible. AI generation, lead management, campaign analytics, and reward fulfillment are proposed features; they are not implemented on this page. No prizes are offered by this demo.

The Engagement Hypothesis

The concept explores whether retries, completion, and time spent playing could help marketers understand campaign engagement. These signals would need validation; repeated play alone does not establish purchase intent.

Illustrative campaign mockups only: no active offers, prizes, or measured campaign results. The values below are sample dashboard data.

  • Short, memorable interactions around a brand offer
  • Potential engagement signals: retries, completion, time spent playing
  • A hypothesis to test: stronger brand recall through play
