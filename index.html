About Makoplay

Makoplay was born from a simple truth: people don't want to fill forms, they want to win.

We turn marketing into a playable experience. With one click, our AI builds a mini-game around your offer — quick, branded, and fun. Players compete for a prize, submit their info to join, and the first winners claim the reward. When the winner cap is reached, the game ends automatically.

Why it works is human nature. A "chance to win" triggers excitement, curiosity, and competition — the same psychology that makes gaming addictive. And for brands, that means higher engagement, richer leads, and lower acquisition costs than any ad campaign.

Why This Works

We gauge interest intensity from retries and play patterns. If someone plays 100 times to win 100 pizzas, they’re a pizza super-fan. If someone keeps trying to win an iPhone, they have high purchase intent for that device. We pass this signal with every lead.

Retries and play patterns are passed as intent signals with each lead.

  • Click-baity campaigns most people can’t ignore
  • Leads with high-intent data (retries, completion, time-on-game)
  • High engagement & brand recognition through play
